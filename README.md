# Killer Kube

## Game summary
The advancement of technology has rendered humans more and more useless, beginning with the creation of basic AIs. A technological boom occurred in 2100 that led to the creation of entities exactly mirroring humans: androids.
As time went on, it was difficult for anyone to distinguish flesh from metal, natural or artificial. The mass production of androids were exploited by corprorations causing almost all humans to lose their jobs.
Corporations made it their number one goal to sway the goverment in putting law that considered androids as humans. They succeeded. As a result, without a method to compensate the now jobless populic, society drowned in chaos.
The once normal streets were now littered with violence, wiping away any culture that once lived. Many humans shared great disdain towards the androids, while a small fraction still considered them human. No matter the person, everyone was united
with hatred against the self serving, self sufficient corporations. 

A small group of humans who still muster affection towards innocent androids stumbled upon a small factory, a factory for producing androids. It seemed to be where the production of androids began due to the older models of androids present.
This is where the small group will use androids for good: building a safe society for today, and cleansing evil for tomorrow. Androids will be created and used to search for supplies in the world to prevent any humans from being hurt or killed. After all,
the factory can be used to create as many androids as necessary. One day, maybe the small group will have a chance against the puppet masters of today's apocalypse.
